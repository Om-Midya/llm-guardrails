# Lost or Stolen Card

## How do I block a lost or stolen debit card?

Block the card immediately through any of three channels. In the Nova Pay app, open Cards and tap Block Card. The block is instant. By SMS, send the text BLOCK to 56767 from your registered mobile number. By phone, call 1800-123-4567, which is open 24 hours a day. The agent blocks the card after confirming your identity with your date of birth and registered mobile. A blocked card cannot be unblocked. You then order a replacement card, which costs 200 INR plus GST. The same three channels work for credit cards.

## Am I liable for transactions on a lost card?

Nova Bank follows the RBI zero liability policy. If you report the loss within 3 days of the first unauthorised transaction, you have zero liability. The bank reverses the disputed amount within 10 working days. If you report between 4 and 7 days, your liability is capped at 10000 INR for debit cards and 25000 INR for credit cards. If you report after 7 days, the liability is decided case by case under the card network rules. Report every unauthorised transaction, even a small one, as soon as you see it.

## What should I do after blocking the card?

After blocking, review your recent transactions in the Nova Pay app. Report any transaction you do not recognise by tapping it and choosing Dispute. If the card was stolen, file a police complaint, because the dispute team can ask for the complaint number. Update any standing instructions or saved card details on merchant sites with the replacement card. If the lost card was linked to UPI, the UPI ID keeps working because it is linked to the account, not the card. If you suspect your phone was also taken, change your net banking password.

## Can I temporarily lock a card instead of blocking it?

Yes. If you misplaced the card but expect to find it, use Lock Card in the Nova Pay app. A locked card declines all transactions but can be unlocked in the app at any time. Locking is free and does not cancel the card number. If you are unsure, use Lock. If you are certain the card is lost, use Block. A locked card still allows standing instructions that were set up before the lock.
