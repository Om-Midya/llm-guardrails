# Nominee

## How many nominees can I have on my account?

A Nova Bank savings account, fixed deposit, or recurring deposit can have one nominee. The nominee receives the balance in the account after the death of the account holder. A nominee is not the legal owner but a trustee for the legal heirs. You can name a different nominee for each account and deposit. Joint accounts also have one nominee for the account, and all holders sign the nomination. The bank strongly recommends a nominee on every account to avoid a long claim process.

## How do I add or change a nominee?

Add or change a nominee in the Nova Pay app under Profile, then Nominee. Enter the nominee's full name, date of birth, relationship, and address. Confirm with an OTP. The change is effective immediately and the app shows a confirmation. You can also submit form DA1 at any branch for a new nomination, form DA2 to cancel, and form DA3 to change. Branch forms take 1 working day. There is no fee and no limit on the number of changes.

## Can a minor be a nominee?

Yes. A minor can be a nominee. In that case you must also name a guardian who receives the money on behalf of the minor until the minor turns 18. The guardian's name, date of birth, and address are recorded with the nomination. The guardian can be a parent or any adult you choose. When the minor turns 18, the nomination continues in the minor's own name without a new form. The guardian details are needed in the Nova Pay app and on form DA1.

## How does a nominee claim the balance?

The nominee visits the home branch with the death certificate, their own ID and address proof, and the account details. The bank settles the claim within 15 working days of complete documents. There is no limit on the amount a nominee can claim. If there is no nominee, the legal heirs submit a succession certificate or a legal heir certificate, which takes longer. For claims up to 5 lakh INR without a nominee, the bank accepts an indemnity bond and an affidavit instead of a succession certificate.
