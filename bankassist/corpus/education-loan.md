# Education Loan

## How much can I borrow for education?

Nova Bank education loans cover up to 20 lakh INR for courses in India. For courses abroad, the limit is 40 lakh INR. Loans up to 7.5 lakh INR need no collateral. Loans above 7.5 lakh INR need collateral such as property or a fixed deposit worth at least the loan amount. A parent or guardian is a co-borrower on every education loan. The loan covers tuition, hostel, books, laptop, exam fees, and travel for courses abroad. The bank pays tuition directly to the institution each semester.

## When do I start repaying?

Repayment starts after the moratorium period. The moratorium is the course duration plus 12 months. During the moratorium, simple interest accrues on the amount disbursed. Paying the interest during the moratorium earns a 1 percent rate reduction for the full tenure. If you do not pay the interest, it is added to the principal at the end of the moratorium. The repayment tenure after the moratorium is up to 15 years. The EMI is fixed at the end of the moratorium.

## What is the interest rate?

The rate for loans up to 7.5 lakh INR is 9.5 percent per year. For loans above 7.5 lakh INR with collateral, the rate is 9 percent. Female students get a 0.5 percent discount. Students admitted to institutions on the bank's premier list get an additional 0.5 percent discount. The rate is floating and resets every 3 months with the repo rate. Interest paid on an education loan is deductible under Section 80E of the Income Tax Act for 8 years.

## Which courses are eligible?

Graduate, postgraduate, doctoral, and diploma courses from recognised institutions are eligible. Professional courses such as engineering, medicine, management, and law are covered. For courses abroad, the institution must be in the bank's approved list, which is available in the Nova Pay app under Loans, then Education. Short courses under 6 months and distance learning courses are not eligible. The student needs a confirmed admission before applying. Sanction takes 7 working days after complete documents.
