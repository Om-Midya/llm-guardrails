# Branch Hours

## What are the branch timings on weekdays?

Nova Bank branches are open from 9:30am to 4:30pm, Monday to Friday. Cash counters close at 4:00pm. Cheque deposits made before 11:00am are sent for same day clearing. Lockers are accessible during the full branch hours. Some branches in airports and railway stations have extended hours, which are listed in the Nova Pay app under Branch Locator. Branch timings do not change during festival weeks unless the day is a declared holiday.

## Are branches open on Saturday?

Branches are open on the first, third, and fifth Saturday of every month from 9:30am to 1:30pm. Cash counters close at 1:00pm on those days. Branches are closed on the second and fourth Saturday of every month. Branches are closed on every Sunday. If a month has a fifth Saturday, the branch is open that day. The Nova Pay app shows a calendar of open Saturdays under Branch Locator. Doorstep banking and the Nova Pay app work on all days.

## Which holidays are branches closed?

Branches follow the state holiday list published under the Negotiable Instruments Act. The list differs by state and is available in the Nova Pay app under Branch Locator, then Holidays. National holidays on 26 January, 15 August, and 2 October apply in every state. On a holiday, NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI, and ATMs continue to work. Cheque clearing does not happen on holidays and resumes on the next working day. Loan EMI debits due on a holiday happen on the next working day with no penalty.

## How do I find the nearest branch or ATM?

Open the Nova Pay app and tap Branch Locator. The app shows branches and ATMs near your current location with distance, timings, and services available. You can filter by services such as locker, cash deposit machine, or forex. The website has the same locator. You can also call 1800-123-4567 and ask for the nearest branch by pin code. Each branch page shows the branch IFSC, the branch manager's name, and the phone number.
