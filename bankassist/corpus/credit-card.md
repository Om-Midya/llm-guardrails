# Credit Card

## How does the credit card billing cycle work?

Nova Bank credit cards run on a 30 day billing cycle. The statement is generated on the same date every month. After the statement date, you get a 20 day grace period to pay. If you pay the full statement balance within the grace period, no interest is charged on purchases. Cash withdrawals attract interest from the day of withdrawal. The statement shows the total due, the minimum due, and the payment due date. You can change the statement date once every 6 months in the Nova Pay app.

## What is the minimum amount due?

The minimum amount due is 5 percent of the total statement balance, or 200 INR, whichever is higher. Paying only the minimum avoids the late fee but not interest. Interest of 3.5 percent per month applies to the unpaid balance. New purchases also lose the interest-free period until the full balance is cleared. The bank recommends paying the full balance every month. Auto-debit for the full or minimum amount is available in the Nova Pay app under Cards, then Auto Pay.

## What is the late payment fee?

If the minimum due is not paid by the due date, a late fee applies. For a total due under 5000 INR, the late fee is 500 INR. For a total due of 5000 INR or above, the late fee is 1000 INR. The fee is charged on the next statement. Late payment is also reported to credit bureaus after 30 days past due. If the previous 12 months were paid on time, the bank reverses one late fee per card per year on request. Call 1800-123-4567 to request the reversal.

## How do I pay my credit card bill?

Pay from any Nova Bank account through the Nova Pay app, net banking, or UPI. Payments from Nova Bank accounts reflect instantly. Payments from other banks through NEFT or IMPS reflect within 1 working day. Use the 16 digit card number as the beneficiary account number and IFSC NOVA0000999 for transfers from other banks. Cheque payments take 3 working days to clear and are not recommended close to the due date. Payments made after 8pm on the due date count for the next day.
