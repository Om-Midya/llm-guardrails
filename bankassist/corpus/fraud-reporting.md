# Fraud Reporting

## How do I report a fraudulent transaction?

Call 1800-123-4567 as soon as you notice the transaction. The line is open 24 hours a day. Report within 24 hours for the fastest recovery. The agent blocks the affected card or channel on the call and files a fraud complaint. You can also report in the Nova Pay app under Help, then Report Fraud. Give the transaction date, amount, and the merchant or beneficiary name. The bank sends a reference number by SMS within 1 hour. Keep the number for all follow-ups.

## What happens after I file a fraud complaint?

The fraud team places a hold on further debits from the affected channel. For card fraud, the bank gives a provisional credit within 10 working days. For UPI and transfer fraud, the bank sends a recall request to the beneficiary bank within 1 hour. Recovery depends on whether the funds are still in the beneficiary account. The team updates you by SMS at each stage. Most cases close within 45 days. You can track the status in the Nova Pay app under Help, then My Requests.

## Do I need to file a police complaint?

For amounts above 25000 INR, the bank asks for a police complaint or a report on the National Cyber Crime Portal at cybercrime.gov.in. The portal report takes about 10 minutes and gives an acknowledgement number. Share the number with the bank through the app or by email to care@novabank.example. For amounts under 25000 INR, a police complaint is optional but it strengthens the case. The bank's fraud team can give you a letter with transaction details to attach to the complaint.

## How does the bank protect me from fraud?

The bank sends an OTP for every online card payment, UPI setup, and new beneficiary addition. New payees have a 24 hour transfer cap. Unusual transactions trigger a call from the fraud team to confirm. You can lock your cards instantly in the Nova Pay app. International usage and online usage are off by default. The bank monitors transactions around the clock. If a transaction matches a fraud pattern, the bank can block it before it completes.
