# Fixed Deposit

## What tenures are available for a fixed deposit?

Nova Bank fixed deposits run from 7 days to 10 years. You choose any number of days within that range. The minimum deposit is 5000 INR for a branch booking and 1000 INR for a booking in the Nova Pay app. There is no maximum. Deposits above 2 crore INR are classified as bulk deposits with separately quoted rates. Interest is paid monthly, quarterly, or at maturity, as you choose at booking. Cumulative deposits reinvest the interest and pay the total at maturity.

## What are the current fixed deposit interest rates?

The rate for a 1 year deposit is 6.5 percent per year. The rate for 18 months is 7.1 percent per year. The rate for 3 years is 7.25 percent per year. Deposits of 7 to 45 days earn 3.5 percent. Deposits of 46 to 179 days earn 5 percent. Deposits of 180 to 364 days earn 6 percent. Deposits of 5 to 10 years earn 7 percent. Senior citizens earn an extra 0.5 percent on all tenures of 1 year and above. Rates are fixed for the full tenure at booking. The bank publishes rate changes 7 days in advance.

## How do I book a fixed deposit?

Book in the Nova Pay app under Deposits, then New FD. Choose the amount, tenure, and payout option. The amount is debited from your savings account instantly. The deposit receipt is available as a PDF in the app within 5 minutes. You can also book at any branch with a filled FD form. Branch bookings need a cheque or a debit from your account. Auto-renewal is on by default. You can switch it off in the app at any time before maturity.

## Is TDS deducted on fixed deposit interest?

If the total interest across all your deposits exceeds 40000 INR in a financial year, the bank deducts TDS at 10 percent. For senior citizens, the threshold is 50000 INR. If your income is below the taxable limit, submit Form 15G in the Nova Pay app at the start of each financial year. Senior citizens submit Form 15H instead. Without a PAN on record, TDS is deducted at 20 percent. TDS certificates are available in the app under Deposits, then Tax Documents.
