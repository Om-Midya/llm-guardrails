# Missed Loan EMI

## What happens if my EMI bounces?

If the EMI debit fails because of insufficient balance, the bank charges a bounce fee of 500 INR plus GST. The bank retries the debit on the 5th and the 10th of the month. Each failed retry attracts another bounce fee. Penal interest of 2 percent per year applies to the overdue EMI amount for the number of days it stays unpaid. The bank sends an SMS on the day of the bounce and before each retry. You can pay the missed EMI any time in the Nova Pay app under Loans, then Pay Overdue.

## When is a missed EMI reported to the credit bureau?

The bank reports the overdue status to credit bureaus after 30 days past the due date. The report shows the number of days past due. A report of 30 days or more lowers your credit score for up to 36 months. If you pay within 30 days, nothing is reported. If you pay after 30 days, the bank updates the bureau as paid in the next monthly cycle, but the history of the delay remains. Three consecutive missed EMIs mark the loan as a special mention account.

## Can I change my EMI date?

Yes. You can change the EMI date once in 12 months. Choose from the 1st, 5th, 10th, or 15th of the month. The change costs 500 INR plus GST and takes effect from the next cycle. Request the change in the Nova Pay app under Loans, then Change EMI Date. Align the EMI date with your salary date to avoid bounces. If the primary account is short, the bank can auto-sweep the EMI from a second Nova Bank account.

## What should I do if I cannot pay for a few months?

Contact the bank before the due date. Call 1800-123-4567 or email care@novabank.example with the loan account number. The bank can offer a 3 month EMI holiday once in the loan tenure for job loss or medical emergency. Interest accrues during the holiday and is added to the principal. The bank can also extend the tenure to reduce the EMI. Either option needs supporting documents and approval within 5 working days. A restructured loan is reported to the bureau as restructured.
