# Account Closure

## How do I close my Nova Bank savings account?

Visit any Nova Bank branch with your debit card, unused cheque leaves, and a valid photo ID. Fill in the account closure form and state where the remaining balance must go. You can take the balance as cash up to 20000 INR, as a demand draft, or as a transfer to another account in your name. If the account has no linked loan, locker, or standing instruction, you can also close it in the Nova Pay app. Open Services, then Close Account. The account closes within 3 working days once all dues are cleared.

## Is there a fee for closing an account?

Closure is free after the first year of the account. If you close within the first year, Nova Bank charges 500 INR plus GST. If the account holder dies, there is no fee. If Nova Bank asks the customer to close the account, there is no fee. Fees are debited from the balance before the final payout.

## What must I clear before closure?

All dues must be settled. This includes any negative balance, pending card charges, locker rent, and loan EMIs linked to the account. Surrender your debit card and any unused cheque book at the branch. Cancel standing instructions and auto-debit mandates such as SIPs and insurance premiums, or move them to another account. If a fixed deposit is linked, break it or move its maturity credit to another account first.

## What happens to my linked services after closure?

UPI handles and net banking logins tied only to the closed account stop working on the closure date. If you hold another Nova Bank account, Nova Pay app access continues. Statements for the closed account remain available on request for 7 years. Any refund or reversal that arrives after closure is returned to the sender, so complete pending disputes before you close.

## Can I reopen a closed account?

A closed account cannot be reopened. You can open a new account with fresh KYC, and the new account gets a new number. Nova Bank keeps your customer ID and your KYC documents on file for 10 years. A new account therefore does not need a full repeat of document collection.
