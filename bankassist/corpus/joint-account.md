# Joint Account

## What operating modes are available for a joint account?

Nova Bank offers three operating modes for joint accounts. Either or Survivor lets any holder operate the account alone, and the survivor continues after the death of the other. Former or Survivor lets only the first holder operate during their lifetime, and the second holder takes over after. Jointly requires all holders to sign every transaction. Choose the mode at account opening. To change the mode later, all holders sign a request at the branch. The mode is printed on the welcome letter and visible in the Nova Pay app.

## How many holders can a joint account have?

A joint account can have up to 4 holders. Every holder submits PAN and Aadhaar or passport. Each holder gets their own customer ID, debit card, and Nova Pay app login. In Either or Survivor mode, each holder has full access. In Jointly mode, net banking transfers need approval from every holder in the app before they execute. Adding or removing a holder needs a signed request from all existing holders and takes 2 working days.

## Who gets the money if a joint holder dies?

In Either or Survivor and Former or Survivor modes, the surviving holder continues the account and the balance belongs to the survivor. The survivor submits the death certificate at the branch, and the bank removes the deceased holder within 3 working days. In Jointly mode, the account is frozen until the survivor and the legal heirs of the deceased submit a claim. The nominee on a joint account receives the balance only after all holders die.

## Can a joint account have a nominee?

Yes. A joint account has one nominee, named with the signature of all holders. The nominee receives the balance after the death of all holders. A nominee is not needed for the survivor to continue the account. Change the nominee in the Nova Pay app, where each holder approves the change, or with form DA3 signed by all holders at the branch. There is no fee for nomination on a joint account.
