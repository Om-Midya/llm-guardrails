# Account Opening

## What documents do I need to open an account?

To open a Nova Bank account, you need your PAN card plus one proof of identity and address. The accepted proof is Aadhaar or a passport. The PAN is mandatory for all accounts. If you do not have a PAN, you must submit Form 60 along with Aadhaar. The bank accepts the documents in original for a branch visit. For video KYC, you show the original documents on camera. A passport-size photograph is not needed for digital onboarding. The bank captures a live photo during the process. All documents must be valid on the date of application.

## Can I open an account online with video KYC?

Yes. Nova Bank offers video KYC through the Nova Pay app and the website. Video KYC is available from 9am to 7pm on all days, including Sundays. A bank officer joins a live video call, checks your PAN and Aadhaar, and records your consent. The call takes about 8 minutes. You need a stable internet connection, a working camera, and your original documents in hand. If the call drops, you can restart it from the same application number within 24 hours. Video KYC accounts have the same features as branch-opened accounts.

## How long does it take for the account to become active?

After the bank completes your KYC, the account is active within 24 hours. You receive an SMS with the account number and customer ID. Net banking and the Nova Pay app work from the moment the account is active. The debit card is dispatched within 3 working days and arrives in 5 to 7 working days. A welcome kit with the cheque book is dispatched on the same schedule. If the account is not active after 24 hours, call 1800-123-4567 with your application number.

## Can a non-resident or a minor open an account?

Non-resident Indians open NRE, NRO, or FCNR accounts, not a regular savings account. See the NRI accounts section for details. Minors above 10 years can open a minor savings account that they operate themselves. Minors under 10 need a parent or guardian as the operator. For both, the guardian submits their own PAN and Aadhaar along with the minor's birth certificate or Aadhaar. Joint accounts need PAN and Aadhaar for every holder.
