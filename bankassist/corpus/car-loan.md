# Car Loan

## What is the car loan interest rate?

Nova Bank car loans for new cars start at 9 percent per year on a fixed rate. Used car loans start at 11 percent. The rate depends on your credit score and the car model. Electric vehicles get a 0.25 percent discount. The rate is fixed for the full tenure. The sanction letter states the rate, the EMI, and the schedule. Pre-approved car loan offers for existing customers appear in the Nova Pay app under Loans and need no income documents.

## How much of the car price does the bank fund?

For salaried customers, the bank funds up to 100 percent of the on-road price of a new car. The on-road price includes ex-showroom price, registration, road tax, and insurance for the first year. Self-employed customers get up to 90 percent. For used cars, the bank funds up to 80 percent of the valuation, and the car must be under 8 years old at loan end. Accessories above 25000 INR are not funded. The dealer receives the payment directly from the bank.

## What is the maximum tenure?

The maximum tenure for a new car loan is 7 years. For a used car loan, it is 5 years, subject to the car age limit. Shorter tenures get a 0.25 percent lower rate. Your EMI, together with existing EMIs, cannot exceed 55 percent of your net monthly income. The loan can be prepaid in full after 6 EMIs with a charge of 3 percent of the outstanding principal. After 24 EMIs, there is no prepayment charge. Part prepayment is allowed twice a year.

## What documents are needed for a car loan?

Salaried applicants submit PAN, Aadhaar, 3 months salary slips, and 6 months bank statements. Self-employed applicants submit PAN, Aadhaar, 2 years income tax returns, and 12 months bank statements. All applicants submit the proforma invoice from the dealer and a driving licence. Sanction takes 1 working day for pre-approved customers and 3 working days for others. The bank holds a hypothecation on the car until the loan is closed. The hypothecation is removed within 7 working days of closure.
