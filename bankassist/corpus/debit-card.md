# Debit Card

## What are the daily limits on my debit card?

Nova Bank debit cards have a daily ATM withdrawal limit of 40000 INR. The daily point of sale and online purchase limit is 100000 INR. Both limits reset at midnight. You can lower either limit in the Nova Pay app under Card Controls. To raise a limit above the default, visit a branch with your ID. The bank does not raise the ATM limit above 50000 INR for any card variant. International usage has its own cap, described in the international card section. Contactless tap payments without PIN are capped at 5000 INR per transaction.

## How do I activate a new debit card?

A new debit card arrives inactive for safety. Activate it in the Nova Pay app under Cards, then Activate Card. The app asks for the card number, expiry date, and an OTP sent to your registered mobile. You then set a 4 digit PIN. You can also activate the card at any Nova Bank ATM. Insert the card, choose Set PIN, and enter the OTP that the ATM sends to your mobile. Online and contactless payments are off by default. Turn them on in Card Controls after activation.

## What is the fee for a replacement debit card?

A replacement debit card costs 200 INR plus GST. The fee applies to lost, stolen, or damaged cards. If the replacement is due to a bank-side issue such as a chip defect, the bank waives the fee. The replacement card arrives in 5 to 7 working days by courier. You can track the courier in the Nova Pay app. The old card number is cancelled on the day you request the replacement. Standing instructions linked to the old card need to be updated with the new card details.

## What happens if I enter the wrong PIN?

The card is blocked for 24 hours after 3 wrong PIN attempts in a row. The block applies to ATM and point of sale together. The block lifts automatically after 24 hours. If you forget the PIN, reset it in the Nova Pay app under Cards, then Reset PIN. The reset needs an OTP and your date of birth. You can also reset the PIN at any Nova Bank ATM. The bank never asks for your PIN over phone, SMS, or email.
