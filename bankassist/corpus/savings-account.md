# Savings Account

## What is the minimum balance for a savings account?

Nova Bank savings accounts require a minimum average monthly balance. The amount depends on the branch location. Metro branches require 5000 INR. Semi-urban branches require 2500 INR. Rural branches require 1000 INR. The bank averages the balance over the calendar month. If the monthly average stays above the limit, a dip on one day does not trigger a charge. If the average falls short, the bank applies a charge of 100 INR plus GST at the start of the next month. Salary accounts opened through an employer tie-up are exempt from the minimum balance rule. The exemption holds as long as a salary credit arrives at least once every 90 days.

## What interest rate does a savings account earn?

Savings account interest at Nova Bank is tiered by balance. Balances up to 10 lakh INR earn 3.5 percent per year. The portion of the balance above 10 lakh INR earns 4 percent per year. The bank calculates interest on the daily closing balance. Interest is credited quarterly, at the end of March, June, September, and December. The credit appears in the statement with the narration "SB INT CREDIT". Interest earned is taxable under the Income Tax Act. The bank does not deduct TDS on savings interest. You can view the applied rate and the next credit date in the Nova Pay app under Account Details.

## Which branch category does my account belong to?

The bank fixes the branch category at account opening. It depends on the location of the home branch, not on where you live. Metro covers Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad. Semi-urban covers towns with a population between 10000 and 1 lakh. Rural covers all other locations. The category is printed on the welcome letter. It is also visible in the Nova Pay app under Account Details. To move to a different category, submit a branch transfer request at either branch. The new minimum balance applies from the month after the transfer is complete.

## Are there any free services bundled with a savings account?

Every Nova Bank savings account includes a free RuPay or Visa debit card for the first year. It also includes free monthly email statements, free net banking, and free Nova Pay app access. NEFT transfers through digital channels are free. You get 5 free Nova Bank ATM transactions per month. In metro cities, you also get 3 free other-bank ATM transactions per month. Cheque books, physical statements, and SMS alerts are chargeable as per the schedule of charges. Premium savings variants require a higher minimum balance. They offer extra free services such as locker discounts and unlimited ATM withdrawals.
