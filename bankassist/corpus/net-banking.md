# Net Banking

## How do I register for net banking?

Register on the Nova Bank website by choosing New User. Enter your customer ID, which is printed on the welcome letter and the passbook. Enter your debit card number, expiry date, and PIN. The site sends an OTP to your registered mobile number. Set a login password and a transaction password. Both passwords must be 8 to 16 characters with a letter, a number, and a symbol. Registration is instant. If you do not have a debit card, visit a branch to collect a net banking kit with a temporary password.

## How do I reset my net banking password?

On the login page, choose Forgot Password. Enter your customer ID and the OTP sent to your registered mobile. Then enter your debit card number, expiry date, and PIN to prove identity. Set a new password. The reset is instant. If your debit card is also lost, use Aadhaar OTP instead of the card details. If neither option works, visit a branch with your ID and the branch resets the password on the spot. The bank never emails or texts you a password.

## What happens after wrong login attempts?

Net banking locks after 5 wrong password attempts in a row. The lock lasts 24 hours. You can unlock earlier by using Forgot Password and setting a new password. A lock does not affect the Nova Pay app or your debit card. The bank sends an SMS after the third wrong attempt as a warning. If you did not make the attempts, change your password immediately and call 1800-123-4567 to report it.

## Which services are available in net banking?

Net banking covers balance and statements, NEFT, RTGS, and IMPS transfers, and bill payments. It also covers deposit booking, cheque book requests, stop payment, nominee updates, and tax payments. Loan statements and credit card statements are also available. Some services such as address change need an upload of proof and are completed within 2 working days. Net banking has a session timeout of 10 minutes of inactivity.
