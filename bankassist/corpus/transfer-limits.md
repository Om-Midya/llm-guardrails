# Transfer Limits

## What is my daily net banking transfer limit?

The default daily transfer limit for net banking and the Nova Pay app is 1000000 INR per customer. The limit covers NEFT, RTGS, and IMPS together. UPI has its own separate limit of 100000 INR per day. The daily limit resets at midnight. Transfers to your own Nova Bank accounts do not count against the limit. The Nova Pay app shows the amount used and the amount remaining for the day under Transfers, then Limits. Scheduled transfers count against the limit on the day they execute.

## How do I raise my transfer limit?

You can raise the daily limit up to 2500000 INR in the Nova Pay app. Go to Transfers, then Limits, then Modify. The change needs an OTP and your debit card PIN or Aadhaar OTP. An increase takes effect after a 4 hour cooling period. A decrease takes effect immediately. For limits above 2500000 INR, submit a request at your home branch with a signed form. Branch-approved limits are reviewed every 12 months. Business accounts have a separate limit structure set at onboarding.

## Is there a limit for newly added beneficiaries?

Yes. A new NEFT, RTGS, or IMPS beneficiary has a cap for the first 24 hours. Transfers to that beneficiary are capped at 50000 INR in total during that window. The cap protects you from fraud right after a beneficiary is added. After 24 hours, the normal daily limit applies. You can add up to 10 new beneficiaries per day. The cap does not apply to transfers between your own Nova Bank accounts. Beneficiary addition needs an OTP and is confirmed by SMS.

## Why was my transfer rejected for exceeding the limit?

If the transfer plus the transfers already made today exceeds the daily limit, the bank rejects it. If the beneficiary was added in the last 24 hours and the cap is reached, the bank also rejects it. The Nova Pay app shows the exact reason under Transfers, then History. Wait for the limit to reset at midnight or lower the amount. If you need to transfer a large amount urgently, use RTGS from a branch. The branch manager can approve a one-time higher limit after ID verification.
