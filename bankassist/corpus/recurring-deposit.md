# Recurring Deposit

## What is the minimum monthly installment for a recurring deposit?

A Nova Bank recurring deposit starts at 500 INR per month. You can choose any amount above that in multiples of 100 INR. The installment amount is fixed for the full tenure. The installment is debited automatically from your linked savings account on the date you choose. If the date falls on a holiday, the debit happens on the next working day with no penalty. You can open more than one recurring deposit with different amounts and dates.

## What tenures are available?

Recurring deposits run from 6 months to 10 years, in steps of 3 months. The interest rate is the fixed deposit rate for the same tenure on the day of opening. For example, an 18 month recurring deposit earns 7.1 percent per year. Senior citizens earn an extra 0.5 percent on tenures of 1 year and above. The rate is locked for the full tenure. Interest is compounded quarterly and paid with the principal at maturity.

## What happens if I miss an installment?

A missed installment attracts a fee of 10 INR per 1000 INR of the installment amount per month of delay. For a 5000 INR installment, the fee is 50 INR per month. You can pay the missed installment any time before the next due date in the Nova Pay app. If you miss 6 installments in a row, the bank closes the deposit. It pays the balance with interest at the applicable rate for the period run. The bank sends an SMS reminder 2 days before each due date and after each missed one.

## Can I close a recurring deposit early?

Yes. You can close it any time after 3 months. The interest is paid at the rate for the period run, minus 1 percent as a premature closure penalty. Deposits of less than 5 lakh INR in total installments have no penalty. Close the deposit in the Nova Pay app under Deposits, then Close RD. The amount is credited to your savings account instantly. Partial withdrawal is not available on recurring deposits. A loan against the deposit is available for up to 90 percent of the balance.
