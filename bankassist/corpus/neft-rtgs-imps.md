# NEFT, RTGS, and IMPS

## How does NEFT work and what does it cost?

NEFT transfers at Nova Bank are free through net banking and the Nova Pay app. NEFT settles in half hourly batches, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. A transfer submitted at 10:05 is included in the 10:30 batch and usually reaches the beneficiary by 11:00. There is no minimum or maximum amount for NEFT through digital channels, subject to your daily transfer limit. NEFT at a branch counter costs 2.5 INR plus GST for amounts up to 10000 INR and 25 INR plus GST above 2 lakh INR. The beneficiary bank credits the amount within 2 hours of the batch.

## When should I use RTGS?

RTGS is for large value transfers that need to settle immediately. The minimum RTGS amount is 200000 INR. There is no maximum, subject to your daily transfer limit. RTGS is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The transfer settles in real time, usually within 30 minutes. RTGS through net banking and the Nova Pay app is free. RTGS at a branch counter costs 25 INR plus GST up to 5 lakh INR. Above 5 lakh INR it costs 50 INR plus GST. Use RTGS for property payments, large vendor payments, and investment transfers.

## How does IMPS differ from NEFT?

IMPS is instant and works 24 hours a day on all days. The maximum IMPS amount is 500000 INR per transaction. IMPS costs 5 INR plus GST per transaction through all channels. The beneficiary is credited within seconds. Use IMPS for urgent transfers below 5 lakh INR. For amounts above 5 lakh INR, use RTGS. For non-urgent transfers, use NEFT, which is free. IMPS needs the beneficiary account number and IFSC, or the beneficiary mobile number and MMID.

## What happens if a transfer fails?

If the beneficiary bank rejects a NEFT, RTGS, or IMPS transfer, the amount returns to your account. NEFT returns arrive within 2 hours of the next batch. RTGS returns arrive within 1 hour. IMPS returns arrive immediately or within 1 working day at most. Common rejection reasons are a wrong account number, a closed beneficiary account, or a frozen beneficiary account. If the amount has not returned in 2 working days, raise a dispute in the Nova Pay app with the UTR number.
