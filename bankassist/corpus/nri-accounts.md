# NRI Accounts

## What is an NRE account?

An NRE account is a rupee savings account for non-resident Indians. It holds income earned outside India. Interest earned on an NRE account is tax free in India. Both the principal and the interest are fully repatriable, which means you can move the money abroad at any time without a limit. Deposits come from abroad through wire transfer or from another NRE account. You cannot deposit income earned in India into an NRE account. The account can be held jointly with another NRI or with a resident relative on a former or survivor basis.

## What is an NRO account?

An NRO account is a rupee savings account for income earned in India, such as rent, dividends, pension, or sale proceeds. Interest on an NRO account is taxed in India at 30 percent plus surcharge and cess as TDS. Repatriation is allowed up to 1 million USD per financial year with a chartered accountant certificate in Form 15CB. An NRO account can be held jointly with a resident Indian. If a resident account holder becomes a non-resident, the bank converts the account to an NRO account.

## What is an FCNR deposit?

An FCNR deposit is a fixed deposit held in foreign currency. Nova Bank offers FCNR deposits in USD, GBP, and EUR. Tenures run from 1 year to 5 years. Interest is tax free in India and the full amount is repatriable. The deposit carries no exchange rate risk because it stays in the foreign currency. Interest rates are published separately for each currency and tenure in the Nova Pay app under Deposits, then FCNR. The minimum deposit is 1000 units of the currency.

## How do I open an NRI account?

Apply online on the Nova Bank website or through the Nova Pay app. You need a passport, a valid visa or residence permit, an overseas address proof, PAN or Form 60, and a recent photograph. Documents can be self-attested and uploaded. The bank completes verification through video KYC within 3 working days. Account activation takes 2 working days after verification. Nova Bank branches in India also accept walk-in applications from NRIs visiting India with the same documents.
