# International Card Usage

## How do I enable my card for international use?

International transactions are off by default on all Nova Bank cards. To enable them, open the Nova Pay app, go to Cards, then Card Controls, and switch on International Usage. The change takes effect within 5 minutes. You can set the switch to turn off automatically after a chosen number of days, which is useful for a short trip. The same screen lets you enable or disable ATM, point of sale, online, and contactless usage separately for international transactions. Enable international usage before you travel, because the app needs an OTP sent to your Indian mobile number.

## What is the foreign currency markup?

Nova Bank charges a foreign currency markup of 3.5 percent plus GST on every international transaction. The markup applies to purchases and ATM withdrawals in any currency other than INR. It also applies to transactions billed in INR by a foreign merchant, known as dynamic currency conversion. The bank recommends paying in the local currency to avoid a second conversion by the merchant. The exchange rate is the card network rate on the settlement date. The markup and the converted amount appear as one line in the statement.

## What is the daily international spending cap?

The daily cap for international transactions is 200000 INR equivalent across all channels. This cap is separate from the domestic limits. Within the cap, international ATM withdrawals are limited to 40000 INR equivalent per day. International ATM withdrawals also attract a flat fee of 150 INR plus GST per withdrawal, in addition to the markup. You can lower the international cap in Card Controls. To raise it above 200000 INR for a specific trip, call 1800-123-4567 at least 2 working days before travel.

## Why was my international transaction declined?

Common reasons are international usage not enabled, the daily cap reached, or the merchant category blocked in Card Controls. Some countries are blocked for card usage under RBI and card network rules. The Nova Pay app shows the decline reason under Cards, then Recent Declines. If the reason is not listed, call 1800-123-4567 from abroad on the international line +91-22-1234-5678. Carry a second card from a different network as a backup on long trips.
