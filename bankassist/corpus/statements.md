# Account Statements

## How do I get my monthly statement?

Nova Bank emails a monthly statement to your registered email address for free. The statement arrives on the 2nd of every month for the previous month. It is a password protected PDF. The password is your customer ID. If you do not receive it, confirm your email address in the Nova Pay app under Profile. You can also turn on a weekly email statement in the same screen at no cost. Statements include every debit, credit, charge, and interest entry with the narration and the running balance.

## How much does a physical statement cost?

A physical statement mailed to your registered address costs 100 INR plus GST per request. Request it in the Nova Pay app under Services, then Physical Statement, or at any branch. It arrives in 7 working days. A statement printed at a branch counter costs 100 INR plus GST as well. Passbook updates at a branch or a passbook kiosk are free. Senior citizen accounts get one free physical statement per quarter.

## How far back can I download statements?

You can download statements for the last 7 years in the Nova Pay app and in net banking for free. Choose any date range within those 7 years. Formats available are PDF, Excel, and CSV. Statements older than 7 years are available on request at your home branch. They cost 200 INR plus GST per year of data and take 15 working days. The app also shows a search across all transactions by amount, narration, or payee name.

## Can I get a stamped statement for a visa or a loan?

Yes. A stamped and signed statement is available at any branch on the same day. It costs 100 INR plus GST. Carry your ID and tell the counter staff the date range needed. For visa purposes, most embassies accept the stamped statement for the last 6 months. A balance certificate on bank letterhead is also available at a branch for 100 INR plus GST. Both documents can be issued in INR or with a USD equivalent at the day's rate.
