# Credit Card Rewards

## How many reward points do I earn per transaction?

Nova Bank credit cards earn 2 reward points for every 100 INR spent on regular purchases. Dining spends earn 4 points per 100 INR. The dining category covers restaurants, cafes, and food delivery apps coded as dining by the card network. Fuel, rent, wallet loads, and government payments earn no points. Points are credited within 3 working days of the transaction posting. The Nova Pay app shows the points earned per transaction under Cards, then Rewards. Points are rounded down to the nearest whole number per transaction.

## When do my reward points expire?

Reward points expire 24 months after the month in which they were earned. Points earned in January 2026 expire at the end of January 2028. The Nova Pay app shows the points that expire in the next 90 days under Rewards, then Expiring Soon. The bank sends an SMS 30 days before a batch of points expires. Expired points cannot be reinstated. If the card is closed, all unredeemed points lapse on the closure date. Points do not transfer between cards except on an upgrade to a higher variant.

## How do I redeem reward points?

Redeem points in the Nova Pay app under Rewards. Each point is worth 0.25 INR as a statement credit. A minimum of 1000 points is needed for a statement credit. Points can also be used in the rewards catalogue for vouchers and merchandise. Catalogue redemptions have no minimum. Statement credits post within 2 working days. Voucher codes arrive by email within 24 hours. Redemption requests cannot be cancelled after submission.

## Are there any bonus point offers?

The bank runs seasonal bonus offers that are listed in the Nova Pay app under Offers. Bonus points from offers are credited within 60 days of the offer end date. A milestone bonus of 5000 points applies every year on spending 3 lakh INR in the card anniversary year. The milestone bonus is credited within 30 days of crossing the threshold. Offers from merchant partners are subject to the partner's terms.
