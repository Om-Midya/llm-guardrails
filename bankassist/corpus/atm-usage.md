# ATM Usage

## How many free ATM transactions do I get per month?

Nova Bank savings accounts get 5 free transactions per month at Nova Bank ATMs. At other bank ATMs, you get 3 free transactions per month in metro cities. In non-metro cities, you get 5 free transactions per month at other bank ATMs. Both cash withdrawals and non-financial transactions such as balance enquiry and mini statement count toward the free limit. The count resets on the first of every month. The Nova Pay app shows the free transactions used so far under Cards, then ATM Usage.

## What is the charge after the free transactions are used?

After the free transactions are used, each withdrawal costs 21 INR plus GST. Each non-financial transaction such as a balance enquiry costs 8.5 INR plus GST. The charge applies at both Nova Bank and other bank ATMs. The charge is debited to your account on the day of the transaction with the narration "ATM CHG". Failed withdrawals due to insufficient balance at other bank ATMs also attract a charge of 20 INR plus GST. To avoid charges, use the Nova Pay app for balance enquiry, which is free and unlimited.

## What is the withdrawal limit at an ATM?

The daily ATM withdrawal limit is 40000 INR per card. A single withdrawal is limited to 10000 INR at most Nova Bank ATMs and 20000 INR at ATMs marked High Value. Other bank ATMs set their own per transaction cap, often 10000 INR. The limit resets at midnight. You can lower the daily limit in the Nova Pay app under Card Controls. Cardless cash withdrawal through the Nova Pay app is capped at 10000 INR per day and works only at Nova Bank ATMs.

## How does cardless cash withdrawal work?

Open the Nova Pay app, go to Services, then Cardless Cash. Enter the amount and confirm with your app PIN. The app gives a 6 digit code valid for 30 minutes. At any Nova Bank ATM, choose Cardless Cash, enter your registered mobile number and the code, and collect the cash. The daily cap is 10000 INR. Cardless withdrawals count toward the 5 free Nova Bank ATM transactions. If the code expires, the amount is released back to your account immediately.
