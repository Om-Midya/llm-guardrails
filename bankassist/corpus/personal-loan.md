# Personal Loan

## What is the personal loan interest rate?

Nova Bank personal loans carry a fixed rate between 10.5 and 16 percent per year. The rate depends on your credit score, employer category, and existing relationship with the bank. Salaried customers with a Nova Bank salary account and a credit score above 750 get the lowest rate. Self-employed customers start at 12.5 percent. The rate is fixed for the full tenure. The sanction letter states the rate, the EMI, and the total interest payable. There are no hidden charges beyond the processing fee.

## How much can I borrow and for how long?

The maximum personal loan amount is 25 lakh INR. The minimum is 50000 INR. Tenure runs from 12 to 60 months. Your EMI, together with existing EMIs, cannot exceed 55 percent of your net monthly income. Pre-approved offers for existing customers appear in the Nova Pay app under Loans and are disbursed within 10 minutes. New customers get a decision within 2 working days after submitting documents. Disbursement is to your Nova Bank account or to any other bank account by NEFT.

## Can I prepay a personal loan?

Yes. Prepayment is free after 12 EMIs. Before 12 EMIs, a prepayment charge of 4 percent of the outstanding principal applies. Part prepayment is allowed once per quarter with a minimum of 2 EMIs worth of principal. Part prepayment reduces the tenure by default. You can choose to reduce the EMI instead in the Nova Pay app. Prepayment through the app is instant. A foreclosure letter and a no dues certificate are available in the app within 7 working days of full repayment.

## What are the fees on a personal loan?

The processing fee is 2 percent of the loan amount plus GST, with a minimum of 1000 INR. The fee is deducted from the disbursed amount. A bounced EMI costs 500 INR plus GST. Penal interest of 2 percent per year applies to the overdue amount for the days of delay. A change of EMI date costs 500 INR plus GST. Statement and repayment schedule downloads in the app are free. There is no fee for loan closure after 12 EMIs.
