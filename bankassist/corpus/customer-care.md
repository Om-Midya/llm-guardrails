# Customer Care

## How do I contact Nova Bank customer care?

Call 1800-123-4567. The line is toll free and open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. For written requests, email care@novabank.example from your registered email address. You can also raise a request in the Nova Pay app under Help, then Raise a Request. Branch staff can log the same request for you during branch hours. Keep your customer ID ready. Nova Bank staff never ask for your OTP, PIN, or full card number on any channel.

## How long does customer care take to respond?

Phone calls are answered on the spot. Email and app requests receive a reference number within 1 hour. A first response arrives within 2 working days. Most requests close within 7 working days. Card disputes and fraud cases follow their own timelines, which are listed in the dispute and fraud reporting pages.

## How do I escalate a complaint?

If your request is not resolved within 7 working days, escalate it to the nodal officer. Email nodal@novabank.example with your reference number. The nodal officer replies within 5 working days. If you are still not satisfied after 30 days from the first complaint, you can approach the Banking Ombudsman. Nova Bank shares the ombudsman details in the escalation reply.

## Which services are available on the phone line?

The phone line can block a card, reset a net banking password, and report fraud. It can also raise a transaction dispute, request a statement, update a mobile number after verification, and book a branch appointment. The phone line cannot change your registered address or add a nominee. Those two changes need a branch visit or the app with Aadhaar based verification.

## Is there a separate line for senior citizens and premium customers?

Senior citizens press 1 after the language selection to reach a priority queue. Premium account holders have a relationship manager whose direct number is printed on the welcome kit. Both groups can also book doorstep service through the main number.
