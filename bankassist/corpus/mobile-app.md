# Nova Pay Mobile App

## How do I set up the Nova Pay app?

Download Nova Pay from the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store. Open the app and allow it to send one SMS to confirm your registered mobile number. The SIM must be in the phone you are using. Enter your customer ID or debit card details and the OTP. Set a 4 digit app PIN. The app then offers biometric login with fingerprint or face recognition. Setup takes about 5 minutes. The app works on Android 9 and above and iOS 14 and above.

## How does device binding work?

Nova Pay binds to one device per user. The binding links your customer ID to the phone's unique identifier and the SIM. If you change your phone, install the app on the new phone and complete the SMS and OTP steps again. The old device is signed out automatically. If you change your SIM but keep the phone, the app asks you to confirm the new SIM by SMS. You cannot use Nova Pay on two phones at the same time. A tablet counts as a second device.

## Is biometric login safe?

Biometric login uses the fingerprint or face data stored on your phone by the operating system. Nova Bank never receives or stores your biometric data. If biometrics fail three times, the app falls back to the 4 digit PIN. Transactions above 50000 INR always ask for the app PIN even with biometrics on. If you add a new fingerprint or face to your phone, the app disables biometric login until you re-enable it with the PIN. This protects you against a person who gains access to your phone settings.

## What can I do in the Nova Pay app?

The app covers balances, statements, UPI, NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, bill payments, card controls, deposits, loans, and service requests. You can block or lock cards, set transaction limits, update your nominee, and raise disputes. Cheque book requests and stop payments are available under Services. The app shows a Help section with the full FAQ and a chat option. The app signs out after 5 minutes of inactivity.
