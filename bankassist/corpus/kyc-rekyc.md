# KYC and Re-KYC

## How often do I need to complete re-KYC?

Nova Bank follows the RBI risk-based re-KYC schedule. Low-risk customers complete re-KYC every 10 years. Medium-risk customers complete it every 8 years. High-risk customers complete it every 2 years. The bank assigns the risk category at account opening based on occupation, transaction profile, and residency. Most salaried and pensioner customers are low risk. You can see your risk category and the next re-KYC due date in the Nova Pay app under Profile. The bank sends an SMS and an email 60 days before the due date.

## What happens if I miss the re-KYC deadline?

After the due date, you get a 30 day grace period. During the grace period, the account works normally. If re-KYC is still pending after the 30 days, the bank applies a debit freeze. A debit freeze stops withdrawals, card payments, UPI, and outgoing transfers. Credits such as salary and interest continue to arrive. The freeze lifts within 1 working day after you complete re-KYC. Standing instructions and EMIs due during the freeze can bounce, so complete re-KYC before the grace period ends.

## How do I complete re-KYC?

If your details are the same, submit a self-declaration in the Nova Pay app. The declaration takes 2 minutes and needs an OTP. If your address or name is different, upload the updated Aadhaar or passport in the app or visit any branch. Video KYC is also available for re-KYC from 9am to 7pm. After submission, the bank updates the status within 2 working days. You receive an SMS that reads "Re-KYC completed" with the next due date.

## Which documents are valid for KYC?

The bank accepts PAN as the mandatory identity document. For address and identity proof, it accepts Aadhaar, passport, voter ID, or driving licence. Each document must be unexpired on the submission date. A utility bill is not accepted as the main address proof. It is accepted only as a supporting document for a current address that differs from the Aadhaar address. For NRIs, the bank also needs a valid visa or residence permit.
