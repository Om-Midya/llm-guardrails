# Password and OTP Security

## Will the bank ever ask for my OTP or PIN?

No. Nova Bank never asks for your OTP, PIN, password, CVV, or full card number over phone, SMS, email, or chat. Bank staff cannot see these details and have no reason to ask. Any call or message that asks for them is a fraud attempt. A fraudster can know your name and account details and still be a fraudster. Hang up and call 1800-123-4567 to report it. The bank also never sends links that ask you to log in to claim a reward, update KYC, or unblock an account.

## How do I recognise a phishing message?

Phishing messages create urgency, such as "your account will be blocked in 2 hours". They contain links to sites that look like Nova Bank but have a different address. They ask you to install an app or share your screen. Genuine Nova Bank SMS messages come from sender IDs that end in NOVABK. Genuine emails come from addresses that end in @novabank.example. The bank never uses personal mobile numbers or WhatsApp to contact you about security matters. Forward suspicious messages to phishing@novabank.example.

## What makes a strong password?

Use 12 or more characters with a mix of upper case, lower case, numbers, and symbols. Do not use your name, date of birth, mobile number, or the word Nova. Use a different password for net banking and for your email. Change your net banking password every 90 days. The bank forces a change after 180 days. Never store passwords in notes apps or share them with family. Enable biometric login in Nova Pay so you type the password less often.

## What should I do if I shared my OTP by mistake?

Act within minutes. Block your debit and credit cards in the Nova Pay app. Change your net banking password and your UPI PIN. Call 1800-123-4567 and tell the agent exactly what you shared. The agent places a temporary hold on outgoing transfers. Review your transactions and raise a dispute for any you did not make. Reporting within 3 days keeps you under the zero liability policy for unauthorised card transactions.
