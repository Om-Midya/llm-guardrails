# Failed Transactions

## My UPI payment failed but money was debited. What happens now?

If a UPI payment fails after the debit, the bank reverses the amount automatically within 5 working days. Most reversals happen within 1 hour. You do not need to raise a complaint during this window. The Nova Pay app shows the transaction status as Pending Reversal until the credit arrives. The reversal appears with the narration "UPI REV" and the original UTR number. If the amount is not back after 5 working days, raise a dispute in the app under Transactions, then Report Issue. The bank then investigates with NPCI and the beneficiary bank.

## My card payment failed but the amount is on hold. When is it released?

For a failed card payment, the merchant or the card network releases the hold within 7 working days. The amount shows as a pending authorisation in the Nova Pay app and reduces your available balance. The hold is not a debit. Once the authorisation lapses, the available balance is restored. If the hold is still present after 7 working days, raise a dispute in the app with the merchant name and amount. The bank follows the chargeback process described in the dispute section.

## My ATM withdrawal failed but cash was not dispensed. What do I do?

If an ATM debits your account without dispensing cash, the bank reverses the amount within 5 working days under RBI rules. If the reversal is delayed beyond 5 working days, the bank pays a penalty of 100 INR per day of delay. Note the ATM ID, the time, and the amount from the receipt or the SMS. Raise a dispute in the Nova Pay app under Transactions, then Report Issue, or call 1800-123-4567. For other-bank ATMs, Nova Bank raises the claim with that bank on your behalf.

## How do I track a dispute I raised for a failed transaction?

Every dispute gets a reference number by SMS within 1 hour. Track it in the Nova Pay app under Help, then My Requests. The status moves from Received to Under Investigation to Resolved. Most failed transaction disputes close within 10 working days. If a dispute passes 10 working days, the case is escalated to the grievance cell automatically. You can also email care@novabank.example with the reference number for an update.
