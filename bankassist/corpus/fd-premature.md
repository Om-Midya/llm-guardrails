# Premature Fixed Deposit Withdrawal

## Can I withdraw a fixed deposit before maturity?

Yes. You can close a Nova Bank fixed deposit at any time after 7 days from booking. Close it in the Nova Pay app under Deposits, then Close FD, or at any branch. The amount is credited to your linked savings account instantly in the app and within 1 working day at a branch. Partial withdrawal is allowed in multiples of 1000 INR. The remaining amount continues at the original rate and tenure. Tax-saver deposits with a 5 year lock-in cannot be closed early.

## What is the penalty for premature withdrawal?

The penalty is 1 percent on the applicable rate. The applicable rate is the rate for the period the deposit actually ran, not the rate for the booked tenure. For example, take a deposit booked for 3 years at 7.25 percent and closed after 1 year. It earns the 1 year rate of 6.5 percent minus 1 percent, which is 5.5 percent. Deposits closed before 7 days earn no interest. The penalty appears as a separate line on the closure receipt.

## Are there cases with no penalty?

Yes. Deposits of less than 5 lakh INR closed after 7 days have no penalty. They earn the applicable rate for the period run, without the 1 percent deduction. Deposits closed to reinvest in a longer tenure at Nova Bank also have no penalty. The new deposit must run at least as long as the remaining original tenure. Deposits of a deceased holder closed by the nominee have no penalty. Senior citizens get the no-penalty benefit up to 10 lakh INR.

## Can I take a loan against the deposit instead of closing it?

Yes. A loan against a fixed deposit is available for up to 90 percent of the deposit value. The interest rate is the deposit rate plus 1 percent. There is no processing fee. The loan is set up instantly in the Nova Pay app under Deposits, then Loan Against FD. The deposit continues to earn its full rate. You repay the loan at any time with no prepayment charge. At maturity, the bank adjusts any outstanding loan against the deposit proceeds.
