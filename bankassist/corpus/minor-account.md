# Minor Account

## Can a minor operate a savings account alone?

Yes. A minor above 10 years can open and operate a Nova Bank minor savings account on their own. The account comes with a debit card and Nova Pay app access. A minor under 10 years can have an account, but a parent or guardian operates it. For both types, the guardian submits their own PAN and Aadhaar along with the minor's birth certificate or Aadhaar. There is no minimum balance for minor accounts. Interest is paid at the regular savings rate.

## What are the limits on a minor account?

A minor account has a daily transaction limit of 5000 INR across ATM, card, UPI, and transfers together. The limit is fixed and cannot be raised. Overdraft and cheque book facilities are not available. International card usage is off and cannot be turned on. The guardian receives an SMS for every transaction. The guardian can lock the card or the account from their own Nova Pay app at any time. The minor cannot add beneficiaries without the guardian's approval in the app.

## What happens when the minor turns 18?

The account converts to a regular savings account after the holder turns 18. The holder completes fresh KYC with their own PAN and Aadhaar within 90 days of the birthday. After KYC, the limits lift and all regular features become available. The guardian's operating rights end on the conversion date. If KYC is not done within 90 days, the account is placed under a debit freeze until KYC is complete. The bank sends reminders starting 60 days before the birthday.

## Can a minor have a fixed deposit?

Yes. A guardian can open a fixed deposit in the minor's name. The deposit is operated by the guardian until the minor turns 18. Minor fixed deposits earn the regular rate. Premature withdrawal is allowed with the guardian's signature and follows the regular penalty rules. Interest earned on a minor's deposit is clubbed with the guardian's income for tax purposes under current law. The deposit continues in the holder's own name after they turn 18.
