# Safe Deposit Locker

## What are the locker sizes and annual rent?

Nova Bank offers three locker sizes. A small locker costs 1500 INR per year. A medium locker costs 3000 INR per year. A large locker costs 6000 INR per year. GST applies on top of the rent. Rent is collected in advance for the full year at the time of allotment and on every anniversary. The rent is debited from your linked savings account. Lockers are available at branches that have a strong room, which is about 70 percent of all branches. The Nova Pay app shows locker availability by branch.

## How many free visits do I get?

Each locker comes with 12 free visits per year. Beyond 12 visits, each visit costs 100 INR plus GST. Visits are allowed during branch hours, from 9:30am to 4:30pm on working days. If both are registered holders, two people can enter the locker room together. The bank keeps a visit register and sends an SMS after every visit. The count of visits resets on the locker anniversary date. Visits are not counted on the day of allotment or surrender.

## How do I apply for a locker?

Apply at the branch where you want the locker or in the Nova Pay app under Services, then Locker. You need an active Nova Bank savings or current account at that branch. Submit PAN and Aadhaar and sign the locker agreement on stamp paper. The bank asks for a fixed deposit equal to 3 years of rent as security for new customers. The deposit earns interest. On surrender of the locker, the bank returns the deposit. Allotment is first come first served, and the app shows your position in the waiting list.

## What happens if I do not pay the locker rent?

If rent is overdue for 3 months, the bank sends a notice by SMS, email, and registered post. After 1 year of overdue rent, the bank can break open the locker in the presence of two witnesses. The contents move to safe custody. Overdue rent attracts a penalty of 10 percent of the annual rent per quarter. The locker is reallocated after the contents are moved. Keep the linked account funded on the anniversary date to avoid this.
