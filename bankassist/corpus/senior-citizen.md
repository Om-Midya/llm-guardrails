# Senior Citizen Banking

## What extra interest do senior citizens earn on deposits?

Customers aged 60 and above earn an extra 0.5 percent per year on deposits. This applies to fixed and recurring deposits with a tenure of 1 year and above. For example, the 18 month fixed deposit rate of 7.1 percent becomes 7.6 percent for a senior citizen. The extra rate applies automatically once the date of birth on record shows the customer is 60 or above. Deposits booked before the 60th birthday keep their original rate until renewal. Savings account interest is the same for all ages.

## What is doorstep banking for senior citizens?

Senior citizens get doorstep banking free twice a month. A bank officer visits your registered address for cash withdrawal up to 20000 INR, cash deposit up to 20000 INR, cheque pickup, and document pickup. Request a visit in the Nova Pay app under Services, then Doorstep Banking, or call 1800-123-4567. Visits are scheduled within 2 working days. Beyond 2 free visits a month, each visit costs 100 INR plus GST. The officer carries an ID card and the app shows the officer's name and photo before the visit.

## Do senior citizens get priority service at branches?

Yes. Every Nova Bank branch has a priority counter for senior citizens and customers with disabilities. Token machines issue priority tokens on request. Senior citizens also get a dedicated line on 1800-123-4567 by pressing 9 after the language choice. The dedicated line has a target wait time of under 2 minutes. Form filling help is available at every branch. Senior citizens get one free physical statement per quarter and free SMS alerts.

## Are there special accounts for pensioners?

Yes. A pension account is a savings account with no minimum balance and a free cheque book. Life certificates can be submitted digitally through the Jeevan Pramaan facility in the Nova Pay app every November. The bank also accepts life certificates at any branch and through doorstep banking. Pension credits arrive on the first working day of the month. Form 15H for TDS exemption can be submitted in the app at the start of each financial year.
