# Cheque Services

## What is positive pay and when is it mandatory?

Positive pay is a fraud control for cheques. Before the cheque is presented, you share its details with the bank. The details are the cheque number, date, payee name, and amount. Positive pay is mandatory for every cheque of 50000 INR and above. Cheques of 50000 INR and above without positive pay details are returned unpaid. Submit the details in the Nova Pay app under Services, then Positive Pay, at least 1 working day before the cheque date. At clearing, the bank compares the details. If they match, the bank pays the cheque.

## How do I stop payment on a cheque?

Request a stop payment in the Nova Pay app under Services, then Stop Payment. Enter the cheque number or a range of numbers. The stop is effective immediately. A stop payment costs 100 INR plus GST per cheque. A stop on a range of cheques costs 100 INR plus GST for the whole range. If the cheque is already paid, the stop fails and no fee is charged. A stop payment stays in force until you cancel it or the cheque becomes stale after 3 months.

## What happens if my cheque bounces?

If a cheque you issued bounces for insufficient funds, the bank charges 500 INR plus GST. The payee's bank also charges the payee a return fee. A bounced cheque for insufficient funds is a legal offence under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act. The payee can send a legal notice within 30 days. Three bounced cheques in a financial year lead to the bank withdrawing your cheque book facility. If a cheque deposited by you bounces, the bank charges 100 INR plus GST.

## How long does cheque clearing take?

Local cheques deposited before 11am clear on the next working day. Cheques deposited after 11am clear in 2 working days. Outstation cheques clear in 3 to 5 working days. You can deposit a cheque at any branch or at a cheque deposit kiosk. For amounts up to 50000 INR, you can also deposit it by photo in the Nova Pay app. The app shows the clearing status under Services, then Cheque Status. A cheque is valid for 3 months from the date written on it.
