# Loan Foreclosure

## Is there a charge to foreclose a home loan?

For a floating rate home loan, there is no foreclosure charge. You can close the loan at any time by paying the outstanding principal and interest up to the date of closure. For a fixed rate home loan, the foreclosure charge is 2 percent of the outstanding principal plus GST. If the fixed rate loan is closed with your own funds rather than a balance transfer, the charge is waived after 5 years. Request a foreclosure statement in the Nova Pay app under Loans. The statement is valid for 7 days.

## What is the foreclosure charge on a personal loan?

A personal loan closed before 12 EMIs attracts a charge of 4 percent of the outstanding principal plus GST. After 12 EMIs, there is no charge. Part prepayment before 12 EMIs also attracts the 4 percent charge on the amount prepaid. The charge is shown on the foreclosure statement before you confirm. Car loans follow a separate schedule of 3 percent before 24 EMIs and nil after. Education loans have no foreclosure charge at any stage.

## How do I foreclose a loan?

Open the Nova Pay app, go to Loans, select the loan, and tap Foreclose. The app shows the outstanding amount, the interest up to today, and any charge. Confirm with your app PIN. The amount is debited from your linked savings account. For amounts above 10 lakh INR, the app asks for an OTP as well. You can also foreclose at a branch with a cheque or a transfer. Branch foreclosures take 1 working day to reflect. Foreclosure requests are processed on working days only.

## What documents do I receive after closure?

Within 7 working days of closure, the bank issues a no dues certificate and a closure letter in the Nova Pay app. For home and car loans, the bank also returns the original property documents or removes the hypothecation. Original documents are released at the home branch against your signature within 15 working days. The bank updates the credit bureau as closed in the next monthly cycle. If any document is delayed beyond the stated time, email care@novabank.example with the loan account number.
