# Fees and Charges

## What does the SMS alert service cost?

SMS alerts for transactions cost 15 INR plus GST per quarter. The charge is debited at the start of each quarter in January, April, July, and October. Alerts cover debits, credits, card transactions, and UPI payments. You can switch to free email alerts in the Nova Pay app under Profile, then Alerts. App push notifications are free and do not need the SMS service. Senior citizen and salary accounts get SMS alerts free. OTP messages are always free. They arrive even with the SMS alert service off.

## What does a cheque book cost?

The first cheque book of 25 leaves is free with a new account. Each additional cheque book of 25 leaves costs 50 INR plus GST. Order a cheque book in the Nova Pay app under Services, then Cheque Book. It is delivered to the registered address in 5 to 7 working days. Premium savings accounts get 2 free cheque books per year. Current accounts have a separate cheque book schedule listed in the current account terms.

## What does a duplicate statement cost?

Monthly email statements are free. A duplicate physical statement costs 100 INR plus GST per request. The physical statement is mailed to the registered address within 7 working days. You can download statements for the last 7 years in the Nova Pay app for free, in PDF or Excel format. A stamped statement for visa or loan purposes costs 100 INR plus GST and is available at any branch on the same day.

## Where can I see the full schedule of charges?

The full schedule of charges is in the Nova Pay app under Help, then Schedule of Charges. It is also available on the Nova Bank website and at every branch. The bank gives 30 days notice by SMS and email before changing any charge. Charges debited to your account carry a narration that starts with "CHG" followed by the charge type. If you see a charge you do not understand, call 1800-123-4567 or email care@novabank.example with the narration.
