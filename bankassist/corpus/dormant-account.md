# Dormant and Inoperative Accounts

## When does a Nova Bank account become dormant?

An account becomes dormant after 24 months with no customer-initiated transaction. Interest credits, bank charges, and dividend credits do not count as activity. A UPI payment, an ATM withdrawal, a cheque, a net banking transfer, or a cash deposit at a branch each count as activity. Nova Bank sends an SMS and an email 3 months before the account turns dormant, and again on the day it turns dormant.

## What stops working on a dormant account?

Debit transactions stop. The debit card, UPI, net banking transfers, cheques, and standing instructions are all paused. Credits into the account continue to work, so salary and refunds still arrive. Interest continues to accrue at the normal savings rate. No dormancy fee is charged at any point.

## How do I reactivate a dormant account?

Visit a branch or use the Nova Pay app. Submit fresh KYC, which means your PAN plus Aadhaar or passport, and a recent photo. Then make one customer-initiated transaction such as a small deposit or a UPI payment. The account becomes active within 1 working day of the KYC check. Reactivation is free. Joint accounts need KYC from every holder.

## What happens if the account stays dormant for many years?

If there is no activity for 10 years, the balance and any unclaimed interest move to the Depositor Education and Awareness Fund. Regulation requires this transfer. The money is not lost. You or your legal heirs can claim it back from Nova Bank at any time with KYC documents. Nova Bank then recovers the amount from the fund.

## Can I avoid dormancy without visiting a branch?

Yes. One small UPI payment or a 100 INR transfer from the account every 12 months keeps it active. You can also set a standing instruction from the account for a recurring deposit, which counts as customer activity each month.
