# Home Loan

## What is the home loan interest rate?

Nova Bank home loans start at 8.5 percent per year on a floating rate. The floating rate is linked to the RBI repo rate and resets every 3 months. Your exact rate depends on your credit score, loan amount, and income profile. Credit scores above 750 get the starting rate. Scores between 700 and 750 get 0.25 percent more. Scores below 700 get 0.5 percent more. Women borrowers and co-borrowers get a 0.05 percent discount. The sanction letter states your rate and the spread over the repo rate.

## How much can I borrow against the property value?

For properties valued under 30 lakh INR, the bank lends up to 90 percent of the value. For properties between 30 lakh and 75 lakh INR, the limit is 80 percent. Above 75 lakh INR, the limit is 75 percent. The value is the lower of the agreement value and the bank's valuation. Stamp duty and registration charges are not funded. Your EMI cannot exceed 50 percent of your net monthly income after existing EMIs. Co-borrower income is added for eligibility.

## What is the maximum tenure and the processing fee?

The maximum tenure is 30 years. The loan must end before the oldest borrower turns 70. The processing fee is 0.5 percent of the loan amount, capped at 10000 INR plus GST. The fee is collected at sanction and is non-refundable. There is no fee for a balance transfer from another bank during offer periods listed in the Nova Pay app. Legal and valuation charges are included in the processing fee. Property insurance is mandatory and is quoted separately.

## What documents are needed for a home loan?

Salaried applicants submit PAN, Aadhaar, 3 months salary slips, 6 months bank statements, and 2 years Form 16. Self-employed applicants submit PAN, Aadhaar, 3 years income tax returns with computation, 12 months bank statements, and business proof. All applicants submit the property agreement, the title chain for 30 years, the approved building plan, and the occupancy certificate for ready properties. Sanction takes 5 working days after complete documents. Disbursement takes 3 working days after legal and valuation clearance.
