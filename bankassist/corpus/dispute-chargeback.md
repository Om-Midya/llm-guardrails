# Disputes and Chargebacks

## How do I raise a dispute on a card transaction?

Raise a dispute within 60 days of the statement date on which the transaction appears. Open the Nova Pay app, go to Cards, tap the transaction, and choose Dispute. Pick a reason such as not authorised, goods not received, duplicate charge, or wrong amount. Attach any proof such as an email from the merchant or a cancellation confirmation. You receive a reference number by SMS within 1 hour. Disputes raised after 60 days are accepted only for fraud cases and are decided under card network rules.

## When do I receive a provisional credit?

For fraud and unauthorised transaction disputes, the bank gives a provisional credit within 10 working days of the dispute. The credit is for the full disputed amount. It appears with the narration "PROV CREDIT" and the reference number. For service disputes such as goods not received, no provisional credit is given until the merchant responds. If the dispute is decided in the merchant's favour, the provisional credit is reversed with 7 days notice. The notice is sent by SMS and email with the merchant's evidence attached.

## How long does a dispute take to resolve?

Most disputes resolve within 45 days. The card network allows the merchant 30 days to respond with evidence. If the merchant does not respond, the dispute is closed in your favour on day 31. If the merchant responds, the bank reviews the evidence and decides within 15 days. Complex cases that go to card network arbitration can take up to 90 days. You get an SMS and an email at every stage. The final decision letter is available in the Nova Pay app under Help, then My Requests.

## What evidence helps my dispute?

For goods not received, attach the order confirmation, the promised delivery date, and your message to the merchant asking for delivery. For a duplicate charge, attach both transaction details and the single receipt. For a wrong amount, attach the receipt showing the correct amount. For not authorised, state where you and the card were at the time of the transaction. A police complaint number strengthens a fraud dispute but is not mandatory for amounts under 25000 INR.
