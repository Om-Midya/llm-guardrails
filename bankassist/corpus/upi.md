# UPI

## What is the daily UPI transfer limit?

The per transaction UPI limit at Nova Bank is 100000 INR. The daily limit is also 100000 INR. The daily limit resets at midnight. You can make up to 20 UPI transactions per day from one account. Both limits apply across all UPI apps linked to the account, including Nova Pay, Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm. Merchant payments and person to person transfers share the same daily limit. Certain categories such as hospital and education payments have a higher per transaction limit of 500000 INR under NPCI rules.

## Is there a lower limit for new payees?

Yes. For the first 24 hours after you add a new payee, transfers to that payee are capped at 5000 INR in total. The cap protects you from fraud in the window right after a payee is added. After 24 hours, the normal limits apply. The cap also applies to the first 24 hours after you register UPI on a new device. Merchant payments made by scanning a QR code are not treated as new payees and do not have this cap.

## How do I set up UPI on the Nova Pay app?

Open the Nova Pay app and tap UPI. The app sends an SMS from your phone to confirm the mobile number linked to the account. The SIM must be in the same phone. Pick a UPI ID, which ends in @novabank. Set a 4 or 6 digit UPI PIN using your debit card number, expiry, and an OTP. If you do not have a debit card, you can set the PIN using Aadhaar OTP. The UPI ID is active immediately. One account can have up to 3 UPI IDs.

## Are there any charges for UPI?

Person to person UPI transfers are free. Merchant payments from a bank account are free. Payments above 2000 INR made from a prepaid wallet to a merchant attract an interchange of 1.1 percent, which the merchant pays, not you. There is no charge for setting up or changing a UPI PIN. Failed UPI transactions are reversed automatically within 5 working days with no fee.
